( 01 ) — Manifesto

Code is craft.
Taste is the multiplier.

Anyone can ship a screen. The work is making it feel considered — every grid line, token and easing curve decided on purpose.

Hello — I'm Vincent. My work runs from a fast-paced startup desk to leading frontend at a Hong Kong fintech scale-up: agile innovation on one side, enterprise discipline on the other, and taste holding the two together.

Design in code, not beside it. Grids, tokens and motion systems are decided in the browser, where the product actually lives — typed end-to-end, tested where it matters, and measured after launch, because the first version is only a hypothesis.

For founders and bosses, that means business outcomes, not just tickets: conversion-focused interfaces, Core Web Vitals in the green, and codebases your next hire will thank you for. Every project starts with the metric it has to move.

For designers and engineers, it means no handoff friction — fluent in both languages, and stubborn about the 16ms frame budget every animation has to earn.
